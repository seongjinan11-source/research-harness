Same physical episode schedule/epsilon and same public parameters. Public parameters identical across seeds; policy seed differs. Last checkpoint only. No target adaptation.

- fixed: CE of000, no policy. One unique forward/group.
- uniform:8 traces sampled uniformly with replacement, mean CE for adapters. At deployment report full uniform8-trace probability mean (cost8), plus one best-global trace selected from fixed dev calibration only. Global trace selection mean CE across dev base graphs/rates, frozen before final evaluation, no final labels. Store all8 dev branch predictions and selection budget. Primary uniform is full mixture; selected global is a separate strong control.
- direct: enumerate all8 traces. Let p_a=product of sequential policy probabilities, L_a=mean query CE, c_a=sum bits. Minimize sum_a p_a(L_a+.01c_a) for phi, with L detached; minimize sum_a stopgrad(p_a)L_a for adapters. Sum p_a=1 checked. Detached state means this is the exact policy gradient for fixed conditional states, not a full derivative of hidden-state features with respect to adapters. Policy evaluation greedy at each actual state.
- group_pg: sample8 traces from current policy, detached per-trace rewards R_a=−L_a−.01c_a. A_a=(R_a−meanR)/(population_sd(R)+1e−6). Policy loss −mean(stopgrad(A_a)*logpi_phi(trace_a)); adapter loss mean L_a. No clipping term or GRPO naming: a single on-policy update has ratio1 and cannot test a clipping effect. Sampled mean losses divide by8; direct sum_a p_a L_a and its actor loss receive NO additional division by8 because p already sums1. Fixed duplicate slots are one identical CE. Backpropagate sequentially to bound memory. Direct exact branch weights can be computed from detached CE and policy read states after per-branch forward/backward, ensuring adapter gradients are weighted by current probabilities without retaining8 transformer graphs.
